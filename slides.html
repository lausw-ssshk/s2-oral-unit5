Openings
👂

Why a good start matters

  • 👀 Make people listen — they decide fast if your talk is interesting.
  • 💬 Talk with your listeners — a question or a short story brings them in.
  • 3️⃣ Then tell them your plan: There are three reasons why…
Openings · A–D

Four ways to start

📱 Topic: my favourite app
😐 A · Plain
Today I want to talk about my favourite app.
👋 B · Hello + clear
Hello everyone. Today I’d like to talk about my favourite app, YouTube.
❓ C · Ask a question
Good morning everyone. Do you use apps every day? Me too. So today I’d like to talk about my favourite app, YouTube.
🎬 D · Make a picture
Hi everyone. Imagine you open your phone and tap one app first — which one is it? Today I’d like to talk about my favourite app, YouTube.

C and D are usually stronger — they ask people to think with you.

Finish in 3 steps

📌

1. Say the main point again

In conclusion… / In short… / Overall, I think…
e.g. In short, YouTube helps me learn and relax.

✅

2. Say you are finished

That’s all I want to say. Thank you.
That’s the end of my presentation. Thank you.
I hope my presentation was interesting. Thank you.

🚀

3. (Optional) Ask them to try

Why don’t you try this app this week?
What will happen if you don’t practise?
How can we use this idea at school?

Useful lines
🗣️

Lines you can copy

🚪 Starting

  • Hello / Good morning, everyone.
  • Today I’d like to talk about…
  • Do you like…? / Do you use… every day?
  • Imagine you… / Suppose you could…
  • There are three reasons why…

🏁 Ending

  • In conclusion… / In short… / Overall, I think…
  • That’s all I want to say. / That’s the end of my presentation.
  • I hope my presentation was interesting. Thank you.
  • Why don’t you try…? / What will happen if…?

Mini practice

📚

Task: Talk about a book (or manga) you like.

Write a short start and a short end.

  • 🚪 Start: Use B, C, or D (hello · question · imagine).
  • 🏁 End: Main point again → say finished → (optional) ask them to try.
  • ⏱️ Keep it short — about 30–45 seconds when you speak.

Example start: “Have you ever finished a book and still thought about it the next day? Today I’d like to talk about…”

Remember
🧠

Quick check

  • ❓ Start well — a question or “imagine…” is better than a flat line.
  • 3️⃣ End in 3 steps — main point → finished → (optional) ask them to try.
  • ✍️ Practise with a real topic (app, book, food, sport…).
